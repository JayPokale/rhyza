To join the Interledger network and be able to send and receive payments, you must add one or more peers to your Rhyza instance. Peering establishes the connections needed for your Rhyza instance to interact with another financial service provider (FSP). The purpose of this guide is to help you set up and manage peers.

## Perform prerequisites

:::note
Peering isn't required unless you want to participate in transactions with another FSP on the Interledger network. For foundational peering concepts, refer to the Peers section of [Interledger Concepts](/overview/concepts/interledger/#peers).
:::

Before adding a peer, you and the financial service provider you intend to peer with must both:

### Run an Interledger connector

Rhyza ILP connectors know how to speak to each other. It is recommended that both you and your peer use Rhyza.

### Agree on an asset

Both you and your peer must agree on an [asset](/overview/concepts/accounting#assets) for the peering relationship. You can set up multiple peering relationships with the same peer based on different assets. At least one asset shared by you and your peer must be added to your Rhyza instance before setting up the peering relationship. For more information, refer to [Assets](/integration/requirements/assets/).

### Exchange static Interledger (ILP) addresses

Your <LinkOut href="https://interledger.org/developers/rfcs/ilp-addresses/">ILP address</LinkOut> is self-assigned during Rhyza setup and stored locally as the `ILP_ADDRESS` environment variable for the `backend` service.

### Communicate a connection endpoint

The connection endpoint will be a URL that the other peer will send packets to.

### Exchange auth tokens for the connection endpoint

Incoming `authtokens` allow you to authenticate that packets sent from your peer originated from your peer's Interledger connector and weren't tampered en route. The outgoing `authtoken` allows your peer to authenticate that received packets originated from your Interledger connector and weren't tampered with en route.

### Agree on a settlement mechanism

The settlement mechanism you both agree to use is what facilitates the transfer of actual funds between you and your peer. Neither Interledger nor Rhyza provide a settlement mechanism.
